FAQ

How accurate is the shade matching?+

We use advanced color science (ΔE calculation) combined with AI vision to compare product swatches against your calibrated skin tone reference. Lighting matters—we'll guide you to get the best shot.

Does it check for my specific allergies?+

Yes. During setup, you select from 20+ common allergens (fragrance, niacinamide, etc.) or add custom ones. Every scan cross-references the ingredient list against your profile.

Can I compare dupes?+

Absolutely. Use "Compare Mode" to scan two products back-to-back. We'll highlight differences in price-per-ml, ingredient safety, and finish to declare a clear winner.

Does it check for ethical and clean beauty standards?+

Yes. REMAKE instantly highlights if a product is cruelty-free, vegan, or contains controversial ingredients like microplastics and parabens, so you can shop safely in alignment with your values.
