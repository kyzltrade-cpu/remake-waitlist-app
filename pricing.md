# Pricing — REMAKE Beauty

REMAKE Beauty is a premium, consumer-focused beauty tech app that scans skincare and makeup barcodes to check for toxic ingredients, hidden allergens, and comedogenic ratings, while delivering 100% accurate AI shade matching.

Our monetization strategy uses a **hard paywall** after onboarding. There is no free tier inside the iOS app; a paid subscription is required immediately to access scanning and shade matching services.

## App Store Subscription Plans (iOS IAP)

These plans are billed directly through Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit / RevenueCat):

### Monthly Premium Pass
- **Price**: $9.99 / month
- **Billing**: Recurring monthly subscription (cancel anytime)
- **Includes**:
  - Unlimited barcode scans for skincare & makeup products
  - Full ingredient safety analysis (toxic chemical & comedogenic checks)
  - Custom allergen alerts (fragrance, niacinamide, silicones, etc.)
  - AI Spectral Shade Matching & Foundation Matcher
  - Science-backed cosmetic dupe detector (drugstore vs. luxury formulas)

### Annual Elite Pass (Best Value)
- **Price**: $49.99 / year
- **Billing**: Recurring annual subscription (saves 58% compared to monthly)
- **Includes**:
  - Full, unrestricted access to all REMAKE features
  - High-priority processing for AI color calibrations
  - Early access to new cosmetic chemistry teardown data
  - All features included in the Monthly Pass
