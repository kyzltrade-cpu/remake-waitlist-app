Shea Butter (Butyrospermum Parkii)

MODERATE COMEDOGENIC RISK
45 Safety Score
3.5/5
★★★★☆

The AI Verdict

While renowned for its deep moisturizing and skin-healing properties, Shea Butter contains high levels of oleic and stearic acids. For acne-prone skin, it can act as a heavy occlusive layer that traps dead skin cells and sebum, leading to closed comedones and breakouts.

Chemical Profile & Risk Factors

Oleic Acid Comedogenic Risk

A heavy fatty acid that can disrupt the skin barrier function and trigger breakouts in individuals prone to fungal acne or with naturally oily skin.

Stearic Acid Pore-Clogging Potential

Provides richness to creams but has a known comedogenic rating, making it risky for those battling severe congestion.

Palmitic Acid Moderate Risk

A saturated fatty acid that contributes to the thick texture but can increase skin congestion when not formulated correctly.

Detailed Specifications

Allergens: Generally Safe (Nut Allergy Caution)
Oily & Acne-Prone Match: Not Recommended
Safety Risk: Low (Natural Extract)
Ethics & Sourcing: Often ethically sourced; look for Fair Trade
