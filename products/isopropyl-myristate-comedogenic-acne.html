The AI Verdict

Isopropyl Myristate is one of the most highly comedogenic ingredients in modern skincare, notorious for causing 'acne cosmetica'. It boasts an extremely high comedogenic rating of 5/5. Although it gives formulas a sleek, sheer, and incredibly smooth texture, it achieves this by aggressively penetrating and sealing the pore lining, binding to dead skin cells, and practically guaranteeing trapped bacteria and closed comedones.

Chemical Profile & Risk Factors

Isopropyl Myristate (IPM) Comedogenic Rating 5/5

An ester of isopropyl alcohol and myristic acid. Used to reduce the greasy feel of heavy lotions, but acts as a hyper-efficient pore blocker. Strongly associated with severe breakouts and textured skin.

Barrier Permeation Enhancer Absorption Multiplier

IPM alters the skin barrier's lipid bilayer, helping other potentially irritating ingredients penetrate deeper into the skin, which can further aggravate existing acne.

Detailed Specifications

Comedogenic Rating: 5 out of 5 (Maximum Risk)
Oily & Acne-Prone Match: Avoid at all costs
Function in Formula: Emollient, Texture Enhancer
Common Replacements: Squalane, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride
