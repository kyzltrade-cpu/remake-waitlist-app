The AI Verdict

Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS) is a highly aggressive synthetic surfactant commonly used to create lather in cleansers. It carries a severe 5/5 comedogenic rating. SLS completely strips the skin's natural lipid barrier, triggering extreme transepidermal water loss (TEWL) and compensatory oil production. For acne-prone individuals, this is a highly toxic ingredient that practically guarantees severe irritation, microscopic barrier tears, and deep cystic breakouts.

Chemical Profile & Risk Factors

Sodium Lauryl Sulfate Comedogenic Rating 5/5

An aggressive lathering agent. It disrupts the stratum corneum, creating vulnerabilities where acne bacteria (C. acnes) can rapidly proliferate and cause deep infections.

Harsh Sulfates High Congestion Risk

By stripping away all natural oils, it forces sebaceous glands into overdrive, leading to rapid re-oiling that gets trapped under the compromised, inflamed surface.

Detailed Specifications

Comedogenic Rating: 5 out of 5 (Maximum Risk)
Oily & Acne-Prone Match: Avoid at all costs
Function in Formula: Surfactant, Cleansing Agent
Common Replacements: Coco-Glucoside, Sodium Cocoyl Isethionate
