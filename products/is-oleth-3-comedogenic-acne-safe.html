The AI Verdict

Oleth-3 has a comedogenic rating of 5 out of 5. It is a highly comedogenic emulsifier that will almost certainly clog pores and trigger inflammatory breakouts for acne-prone skin types.

Chemical Profile & Risk Factors

Oleth-3 RATING: 5/5

An oleyl alcohol derivative that acts as a surfactant and emulsifier. Recognized by dermatologists as a severe comedogenic ingredient that easily penetrates the follicular barrier, trapping sebum and bacteria.

Barrier Risk HIGH

Due to its high lipid solubility and low molecular weight, Oleth-3 disrupts the skin barrier, allowing other potential irritants deeper into the epidermis.

Detailed Specifications

Primary Function: Emulsifier / Surfactant
