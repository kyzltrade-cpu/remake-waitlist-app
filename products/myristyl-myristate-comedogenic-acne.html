The AI Verdict

Myristyl Myristate is highly comedogenic, boasting a terrifying 5/5 rating. It is a synthetic ester commonly used to make creams feel luxurious and velvety. Unfortunately, it completely seals the skin barrier, trapping dead skin cells and excess sebum. If you are acne-prone, using products with this ingredient is practically guaranteeing closed comedones and deep cystic breakouts.

Ingredient Profile & Acne Safety

Comedogenic Rating: 5 Severe Risk

Scores a maximum 5 on the comedogenic scale. It has a high molecular affinity for the skin's lipid barrier, meaning it aggressively clings to pores and blocks oxygen exchange.

Acne Types Triggered Danger

Most famous for causing closed comedones (small, flesh-colored bumps) that eventually rupture into inflamed cystic acne due to trapped bacteria.

Why Formulators Use It Warning

It's an inexpensive emulsifier and emollient. It gives heavy creams a soft, powdery finish, tricking the consumer into feeling like the product has fully absorbed.
