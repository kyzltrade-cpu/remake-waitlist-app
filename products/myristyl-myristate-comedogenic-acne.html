The AI Verdict

Myristyl Myristate is a thick, waxy emollient frequently used to give creams and foundations a velvety, rich texture. Unfortunately, it carries a Comedogenic Rating of 5/5, meaning it is almost guaranteed to severely clog pores. It creates an occlusive seal over the skin that traps dead skin cells, sebum, and bacteria, leading directly to closed comedones and cystic acne breakouts.

Why It Ruins Your Skin

Level 5 Comedogenic Rating

On the standard comedogenic scale of 0 to 5, Myristyl Myristate scores a catastrophic 5. This makes it one of the absolute worst ingredients for acne-prone skin. It aggressively binds inside the follicular canal, blocking the natural flow of oil to the skin surface.

The "Velvet Trap" Effect

Brands love this ingredient because it makes cheap formulas feel thick, expensive, and velvety. However, that velvet texture is essentially a micro-wax coating. Beneath that coating, C. acnes bacteria thrive in an oxygen-deprived environment, multiplying rapidly and causing deep, painful inflammation.

Hidden In "Non-Comedogenic" Products

Because the term "non-comedogenic" is completely unregulated by the FDA, many popular moisturizers and thick cream foundations claim to be safe while heavily utilizing Myristyl Myristate. It takes a molecular-level scanner to catch it before it destroys your skin barrier.
