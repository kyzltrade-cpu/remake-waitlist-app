Bismuth Oxychloride

SEVERE ACNE TRIGGER

Bismuth Oxychloride is a synthetic pearlizing agent widely used in mineral makeup, highlighters, and blushes for a glowing finish. However, it is an extremely common hidden acne trigger. Its heavy, crystalline structure can lodge deep in pores, leading to micro-tears, severe cystic acne, and cystic breakouts for acne-prone skin.

Toxic Ingredients Discovered

Crystalline Micro-Tears Cystic Acne Risk

Bismuth Oxychloride has a sharp, crystalline structure. When buffed into the skin, it creates micro-tears that compromise the skin barrier and introduce acne-causing bacteria.
