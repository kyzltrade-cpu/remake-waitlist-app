The AI Verdict

Ethylhexyl Palmitate is a notorious pore-clogging emollient. Used by brands to make cheap formulas feel expensive and silky, this synthetic ester binds tightly to dead skin cells. With a brutal 4/5 comedogenic rating, it is a primary trigger for closed comedones and deep under-the-skin texture. If you are struggling with unexplained acne, check your makeup for this ingredient immediately.

Chemical Profile & Risk Factors

Comedogenic Profile Grade 4 (High Risk)

Scores a highly comedogenic 4 out of 5. It is extremely effective at trapping debris, sebum, and bacteria inside the follicular lining, quickly resulting in breakouts.

Chemical Origin Synthetic Palm Oil Derivative

Derived from palmitic acid (often sourced from palm oil) and 2-ethylhexanol. Primarily used as a cheaper alternative to silicone to provide artificial 'slip'.

Barrier Disruption Medium-High Risk

While functioning as a skin-conditioning agent, its occlusive nature disrupts the skin's natural exfoliation process, leading to severe hyperkeratosis (trapped dead skin cells).

Detailed Specifications

Comedogenic Rating: 4/5 (High Risk)
Oily & Acne-Prone Match: Strongly Advise Against
Fungal Acne Safe: No - Malassezia Trigger
Primary Function: Emollient / Texture Enhancer
