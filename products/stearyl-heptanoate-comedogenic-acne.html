Stearyl Heptanoate

Comedogenic Danger

A heavy emollient that provides a smooth skin feel but frequently traps dead skin cells, leading to closed comedones.

The AI Verdict

Stearyl Heptanoate is an ester of stearyl alcohol and heptanoic acid, often used in rich creams and moisturizers. With a high comedogenic rating of 4/5, it poses a major threat to acne-prone skin, deeply clogging pores. We highly recommend scanning your products with Remake to ensure they are free from this congesting ingredient.

Feature Breakdown & Safety Checking

Comedogenic Rating High Risk (4/5)

Stearyl Heptanoate has a severe comedogenic rating of 4 out of 5. It sits densely on the skin, contributing significantly to the formation of acne over time by trapping sebum and bacteria inside the follicle.

Barrier Disruption Low Risk

While it may condition the skin barrier initially, its occlusive nature makes it unsuitable for those with active acne or congestion.

Acne Safe NOT ACNE SAFE

This ingredient is definitively not acne safe. Products containing Stearyl Heptanoate are flagged with an immediate warning in the Remake app.
