Carrageenan

Skincare Ingredient Audit

SEVERE PORE-CLOGGING RISK

AI Cosmetic Chemist Verdict

Carrageenan is an emulsifier derived from seaweed that is notoriously one of the most comedogenic ingredients in skincare. It is given a 5 out of 5 on the comedogenicity scale. Found in many 'natural' or 'marine' skincare lines, it is highly likely to cause severe acne breakouts, closed comedones, and skin irritation for those with acne-prone skin.

Flagged Ingredients

Comedogenic Rating: 5/5 Severe Pore-Clogging Risk

Carrageenan has a devastating comedogenic rating of 5, meaning it will almost certainly clog pores and cause acne breakouts for anyone with oily or acne-prone skin.

Skin Barrier Disruption Irritation Risk

Aside from causing breakouts, carrageenan can trigger skin sensitivities and inflammation, leading to redness and further compromising a damaged skin barrier.

Hidden in 'Natural' Skincare Deceptive Marketing

Because it is derived from red seaweed, brands often use carrageenan in 'clean' or 'natural' products, giving consumers a false sense of safety.
