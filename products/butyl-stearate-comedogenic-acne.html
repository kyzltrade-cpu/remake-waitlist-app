Butyl Stearate

Found in dozens of popular moisturizers and foundations, this ingredient is notorious for triggering severe closed comedones and inflammatory acne.

Why It's Harmful

Butyl Stearate is an ester resulting from the reaction of butyl alcohol and stearic acid. While it gives formulas a luxurious, silky slip and enhances spreadability, it is highly comedogenic.

Because it is a heavily emollient ester, it penetrates the pore wall and creates an occlusive seal. For those with oily or acne-prone skin, this traps sebum and dead skin cells, inevitably resulting in deep, painful microcomedones.

Acne Warning

Pore Occlusion High Risk

Forms a barrier over the skin that traps dirt and oil, rapidly accelerating blackhead and whitehead formation.

Oleic Acid Base Acne Trigger

Oleic acid components are known to disrupt the skin barrier in acne-prone individuals and feed malassezia yeast.
