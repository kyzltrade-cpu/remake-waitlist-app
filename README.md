# REMAKE • Skincare Barcode Scanner & AI Face Scan App

[REMAKE](https://remake.beauty) is an iOS mobile application that scans cosmetic and skincare barcodes to detect toxic or comedogenic ingredients, analyze beauty formulas, and provide perfect makeup shade-matching using AI technology.

Visit the live waitlist website at: [https://remake.beauty](https://remake.beauty)

## Core Features

- **Cosmetic Barcode Scanning**: Instantly scan beauty products to detect toxic ingredients or hidden allergens.
- **Acne-Safe Checking**: Check comedogenic ratings and pore-clogging triggers before applying product to your skin.
- **AI Makeup Shade-Matching**: Snap a selfie and let our AI calculate your exact skin hex code and undertone.
- **De-Influencing Engine**: Get brutal, objective feedback on trending viral makeup products.
